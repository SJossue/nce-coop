# NCE Undergraduate Engineering Co-op Strategic Plan

Source: NCE Co-op strategic plan doc (linked in `contacts.md`), provided by Nayelli as onboarding
material. Condensed here for reference; goals/tactics kept close to verbatim since they're the
office's actual planning language.

## 26-27 Academic Year

Shift from last year's foundation-building toward **scaling, refining, and measuring impact**:
student communication and employer engagement enhancements.

### Goal 1: Student engagement, readiness, co-op participation
Streamlined pathways, embedding co-op messaging in academics (ENGR 211, orgs, design teams,
peer ambassadors), stage-specific career advising informed by employer feedback and ENGR 211 to
placement data.
**Assessed by:** engagement with co-op programming, ENGR 211 participation/conversion, placement
outcomes, student satisfaction/readiness.

### Goal 2: Employer partnerships
Expand the targeted employer pipeline with coordinated communications; deepen existing
partnerships (ELEVATE Partner Program, sponsorships, candidate access); increase employer
participation in events/classroom programming using employer feedback.
**Assessed by:** number of employers posting/hiring NJIT co-op students, employer satisfaction,
repeat participation, event participation.

### Goal 3: Optimize program operations and infrastructure
> **Directly relevant to this repo.**
- **Tactic 1 (Streamline Administrative Processes):** "Deploy automated co-op eligibility
  determination, enhance communications, and conduct coordinated annual planning... Push
  continuous workflow improvements to support efficient and scalable operations." This is an
  explicit, named 26-27 organizational priority. The posting-monitoring automation this repo is
  for isn't a side project: it ladders directly to a stated office goal.
- Tactic 2: refine policies/handbooks/FAQs/Experience Form processes, alignment across
  faculty/academic/career advisors.
- Tactic 3: flexible/accessible pathways with Academic Affairs, OGI, Financial Aid, Registrar
  for international/transfer students.
**Assessed by:** co-op participation rates, **processing/approval timelines, data quality and
reporting capabilities**, stakeholder satisfaction, equitable access. (The bolded metrics are
the closest thing to a stated KPI for work like ours; see `questions.md`.)

## 25-26 Academic Year (prior year, mid-year status shown, useful precedent)

Focus: strengthen the whole co-op experience across preparation, eligibility, visibility, and
employer partnerships. Fall actions: market ENGR 211 early, peer mentor engagement, review
eligibility criteria, streamline eligibility/approval process.

### Goal 1: Student portfolio & preparation
Tracking system for ENGR 211/FED 101/co-op-approved students; Co-op Job Search Action Plan
embedded in ENGR 211 + advising; peer mentors (Mitchell Rodriguez hired specifically for this;
same Mitchell whose Handshake permissions Jossue's are being matched to); midterm check-ins.
**Mid-year:** mostly complete/ongoing. Note: "a dedicated student employee to support NCE Co-op
work would be helpful and needed." This is very likely the origin of Jossue's own role.

### Goal 2: Eligibility & structure
> **Directly relevant: this is the existing automation to ask about.**
- Tactic 1: eligibility stays credit-based; students on track at **45 credits** become co-op
  eligible and can apply to Handshake Engineering Co-op postings.
- Tactic 2: the old Co-op Eligibility Survey was sunset, replaced by the "mass co-op eligibility
  process" (student-side labeling, not posting-side).
- **Tactic 3 (COMPLETE/ONGOING):** "Pilot a Co-op approval automation system using Registrar and
  Handshake data for mass co-op approval labeling and communication, piloted for Fall 2025 and
  Spring 26 eligible students and in effect for Summer/Fall 26 eligible students." This is
  automated **student** eligibility labeling (who's allowed to apply), built on Registrar +
  Handshake data integration. It is NOT the same as **posting** eligibility labeling (which
  postings only co-op-approved students can apply to; that's Jossue's manual task), but it
  proves NJIT already has *some* Registrar-Handshake automation infrastructure. Worth asking
  directly whether that pipeline could extend to postings, or whether it's a fully separate
  system/vendor integration Jossue wouldn't have access to. **Added to `questions.md`.**
- Tactic 4: F-1 international student co-op pathways, in progress with OGI/Dean.

### Goal 3: Program visibility & engagement
Marketing campaign, Co-op Student Advisory Group (not yet formed), website maintenance
(complete/ongoing), workshops/class visits/design-team collaboration.

### Goal 4: Employer partnerships
Targeted employer list built from Handshake posting/event/hiring history, this is the employer
side of the Bus Dev sheet work; employers get labeled in Handshake and receive posting-reminder/
event communications. "A more organized schedule could be created as to when the list of
relevant employers are updated and when communications go out." This is an open gap that
overlaps with the Bus Dev revamp task.

### Goal 5: Data, policy, resource infrastructure
> **Closest thing to a stated cadence/metric for the posting-monitoring work itself.**
- Tactic 1 mid-year status: "Engineering related Co-op postings are tracked almost daily; those
  that are specific to Engineering Co-op are labeled for those students; incoming postings are
  promoted to NCE Co-op eligible students; expired postings are followed up on." This confirms
  the cadence Jossue is inheriting: near-daily, not weekly.
- Assessed by: co-op-to-full-time conversion rate, onboarding efficiency, student/employer
  satisfaction.
- Note: "potential ways to combine Co-op hiring data (from Experience Forms) with GDS data could
  be further explored." This is a future data-infrastructure thread, not urgent now.
